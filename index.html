FAQ

Are my photos uploaded? No. Photos, videos and audio are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Do I need an account? No. Sign in with Apple or Google is optional.

What is free? Three cameras (Motorola RAZR V3, iPhone 3GS, Polaroid 600), photos only, reduced resolution and a small watermark. Wayback Pro unlocks every camera, video and library import.

How do I cancel or restore? Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. In the app, use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Settings > Delete Account.
