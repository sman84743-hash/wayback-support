Last updated: 28 October 2026

Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone. Wayback Camera processes every frame on the device. Photos, videos and audio are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

  • Nothing, if you continue without an account.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: an account identifier and, if you share them, your name and email address. These are stored by our authentication provider (Supabase) so we can recognise your account.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive only whether your subscription or purchase is active, never your payment details.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show and capture the old-camera viewfinder.
  • Microphone: to record sound for videos.
  • Photos: to import items you pick and to save items you choose to keep.

We do not sell data, show ads or use third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete Account removes your account from our authentication provider and deletes all photos and videos stored inside the app on this iPhone. Items you saved to the Photos app stay there. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple under Settings > Apple ID > Sign-In & Security.

Children: Wayback Camera is not directed at children under 13.
